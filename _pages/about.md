I am an Associate Professor of Environmental Science at Molloy University. My research uses environmental DNA (eDNA), microbial ecology, and bioinformatics to study biodiversity and ecosystem change in marine and estuarine environments.

My current work focuses on:

- developing eDNA approaches for biodiversity monitoring in New York's coastal bays;
- evaluating ecological responses to oyster restoration in Great South Bay;
- coordinating eDNA methods, data, and monitoring across the South Shore Estuary Reserve; and
- understanding how microorganisms mediate carbon, nitrogen, and sulfur cycling in oxygen-depleted marine waters.

Much of this work is collaborative and includes undergraduate researchers. See [Research](/projects/) for current projects and [Publications](/publications/) for peer-reviewed work.
